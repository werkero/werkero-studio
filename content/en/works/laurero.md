Laurero started from a simple frustration: application advice was either expensive, generic, or dishonest. The product combines official policy data, admission records, and score distributions into one conversational assistant.

The pricing is deliberately accessible — a small credit packs model with a generous free tier — so honest guidance isn't a luxury good.
